    Each doctrine can normally be picked once per battle, and the detachments you tick add extra uses. Picking one for the army uses up a pick; tap it again to undo. Pick your Chapter to see its detachments and characters; an army can only include one Chapter. A unit's own doctrine (from a stratagem or enhancement) replaces the army doctrine for that unit and doesn't use an army pick. “New Command phase” clears the active doctrines but keeps your used picks. “New battle” also restores all picks. Everything is saved in this browser on this device.
